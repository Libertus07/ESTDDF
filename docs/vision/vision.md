# ESTDDF Vision

> Building an open, evidence-driven standard for AI-assisted software technical due diligence.

---

## Why ESTDDF Exists

Software projects are becoming increasingly complex.

Modern applications combine cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, artificial intelligence, security requirements, compliance, user experience, and rapidly evolving development practices. Evaluating these systems consistently has become more difficult than ever.

Today, technical audits often depend on individual experience, preferred tools, or the AI model being used. As a result, the same software project may receive significantly different conclusions from different reviewers or AI systems.

ESTDDF exists to reduce that inconsistency.

Our goal is not to replace engineering judgment.

Our goal is to establish a shared methodology that helps both humans and AI perform software technical due diligence in a transparent, evidence-driven, and repeatable way.

---

## The Problem

Current software audits frequently suffer from several challenges:

- Different reviewers follow different methodologies.
- AI-generated reports are highly dependent on prompt quality.
- Findings are often presented without sufficient evidence.
- Recommendations and verified observations are frequently mixed together.
- Risk prioritization lacks consistency across reports.
- Results are difficult to compare between projects.

These inconsistencies reduce confidence in technical assessments and make long-term decision making more difficult.

---

## Our Vision

We envision a future where software technical due diligence follows a common open standard.

Regardless of whether an assessment is performed by a software architect, a security engineer, a CTO, or an AI system, the evaluation process should follow the same core principles.

ESTDDF aims to provide that foundation.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

ESTDDF is built on a simple belief:

> Software deserves evidence—not assumptions.

Every significant technical conclusion should be explainable, supported by evidence whenever possible, and presented with an appropriate level of confidence.

The purpose of ESTDDF is not to generate more reports.

The purpose is to generate more trustworthy reports.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Our long-term ambition is to establish ESTDDF as an open specification that can be adopted by developers, engineering teams, startups, enterprises, researchers, and AI systems alike.

By providing a common methodology, we hope to make software technical due diligence more transparent, repeatable, and accessible for everyone.

---

## What ESTDDF Is

ESTDDF is:

- An open specification.
- An evidence-driven methodology.
- Technology agnostic.
- AI friendly.
- Community driven.

---

## What ESTDDF Is Not

ESTDDF is not:

- A static analysis tool.
- A security scanner.
- A linter.
- A testing framework.
- A replacement for engineering expertise.

Instead, ESTDDF defines how technical evaluations should be performed and documented.

---

## Our Commitment

We believe that better engineering decisions begin with better technical evaluations.

ESTDDF exists to help create those evaluations through openness, consistency, transparency, and evidence.
