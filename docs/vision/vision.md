# ESTDDF Vision

## Why ESTDDF Exists

Software has become one of the world's most critical infrastructures. Businesses, governments, researchers, and individuals increasingly depend on software systems to operate safely, reliably, and efficiently.

As software systems continue to grow in complexity, evaluating their technical quality has become significantly more challenging. Modern systems combine distributed architectures, cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, security requirements, regulatory constraints, and rapidly evolving technologies.

Despite this complexity, software technical due diligence still lacks a widely accepted, open methodology that enables consistent, transparent, and evidence-driven assessments.

ESTDDF exists to provide that common foundation.

Rather than replacing engineering expertise, ESTDDF defines a shared methodology that can be applied consistently by both human reviewers and AI systems to improve the quality, transparency, and repeatability of technical evaluations.
